Process Lifecycle

Comprehensive Finishing Solutions

Our end-to-end capabilities ensure rigorous quality control from initial substrate preparation to final non-destructive inspection.

Stage 01
Solves: Mill Scale, Rust & Contaminant Removal

Surface Preparation

Abrasive media blasting and multi-stage chemical pretreatment stripping mill scale, rust, and oils to create an exact anchor profile for maximum adhesion.

Standard: SSPC-SP 10 / NACE No. 2 (Near-White)
Example: Heavy crane booms & offshore subsea skids
Stage 02
Solves: Extreme UV & Long-Term Weathering

Powder Coating

High-voltage electrostatic spray and fluidized bed polymer deposition producing uniform, solvent-free coatings with superior mechanical toughness.

Envelope: Parts up to 30' L × 10' W × 10' H
Example: Automotive chassis & architectural mullions
Stage 03
Solves: Heat-Sensitive & Large Structural Units

Liquid Protective Coating

High-performance 2K aliphatic polyurethanes, high-build epoxies, and fluoropolymer wet finishes engineered for thermal-sensitive and oversized geometries.

Application: Air-Assisted & High-Pressure Airless
Example: Refinery valve bodies & chemical pipelines
Stage 04
Solves: ASTM / MIL-SPEC Certified Compliance

Quality Assurance

Full-spectrum non-destructive evaluation verifying dry film thickness (DFT), cross-hatch adhesion, gloss uniformity, and holiday porosity detection.

Testing: ASTM D3359 Adhesion & ASTM D523 Gloss
Example: Batch mil-spec inspection certificates
Core Offerings

Detailed Service Capabilities

Explore our core industrial services, processing specifications, and field-tested use cases.

Our electrostatic powder coating lines provide an ultra-durable, pinhole-free finish that resists chipping, scratching, and chemical degradation. Completely solvent-free with near-zero VOC emissions, this process delivers uniform coverage over intricate welds and sharp stamped edges.

  • Application Range: Automated conveyor & large-scale batch booths (up to 30 ft)
  • Thermal Curing: Convection curing ovens with programmable ±5°F profile zones
  • Substrate Compatibility: Carbon steel, cast iron, galvanized, aluminum & magnesium alloys
  • Finishing Options: High-gloss, satin, matte, fine wrinkle, leatherette & sparkle metallic
  • Corrosion Performance: Up to 3,500 hours ASTM B117 salt spray protection

Real-World Example: 12,000 architectural solar canopy purlins and commercial EV charging stations finished with 10-year UV-warranty super-durable powder.

Flawless coating adhesion begins with mechanical profiling. We operate dedicated blasting enclosures utilizing virgin aluminum oxide, steel grit, glass beads, and garnet to strip heat scale, laser oxide edges, and oxidation, establishing a verified 1.5–3.5 mil anchor pattern.

  • Media Selection: Aluminum oxide, chilled iron grit, garnet, walnut shell & glass bead
  • Surface Cleanliness: Certified SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal), SP 10 (Near-White) & SP 6 (Commercial)
  • Chemical Pretreatment: 5-stage automated wash with iron/zinc phosphate & silane conversion
  • Surface Profiling: Optical micrometer verification of 1.5 - 3.5 mil angular anchor depth

Real-World Example: Total mill-scale de-scaling of structural I-beams and welded pressure vessel shells prior to application of zinc-rich primer.

Beyond corrosion prevention, we engineer the visual, tactile, and ergonomic properties of your assemblies. We control specular gloss with precision from 5% (dead flat) to 95%+ (automotive mirror gloss) while integrating functional textures to enhance grip, mask casting flaws, or meet anti-glare aerospace specs.

  • Gloss Calibration: 60° geometry gloss testing ranging from dead matte to 95 GU mirror finish
  • Tactile Textures: Heavy stipple, fine sand, ripple, hammertone & anti-slip traction grip
  • Masking & Thread Defense: Custom silicone plugs, high-temp kapton masking & threaded caps
  • Precision Assembly Ready: CNC-toleranced mating surfaces kept 100% paint-free

Real-World Example: Textured, glare-free anti-static finish on military flight simulator consoles and medical diagnostic cart housings.

For aggressive environmental challenges—including deep immersion, splash zones, chemical contact, and thermal cycling—we engineer advanced wet chemical systems. Our technicians apply high-solids epoxies, 2K urethanes, vinyl esters, and PTFE coatings designed to withstand continuous exposure.

  • Severe Service Systems: Glass-flake reinforced epoxy, novolac vinyl esters & fluoropolymers
  • Immersion Protection: Potable water approved (NSF/ANSI 61) and crude/fuel chemical containment
  • Extreme Operating Temperatures: Formulations certified up to 1,200°F (650°C)
  • Non-Conductive & Conductive: Specialized dielectric barriers and static-dissipative topcoats

Real-World Example: 3-coat marine epoxy barrier applied to offshore platform riser clamps operating in splash zones (ISO 12944 CX).
