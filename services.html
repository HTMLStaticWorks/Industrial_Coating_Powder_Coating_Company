Core Offerings

Our Service Portfolio

Our electrostatic powder coating lines provide a durable, uniform finish that resists chipping, scratching, and fading. Suitable for a wide range of metal substrates, this solvent-free process is environmentally friendly and delivers exceptional corrosion protection.

  • Electrostatic application
  • Controlled thermal curing
  • Durable thermoplastic/thermoset finish
  • Extensive color flexibility
  • High-grade corrosion protection

Proper adhesion requires a perfectly prepared substrate. Our industrial sandblasting and abrasive media blasting services remove mill scale, rust, and old coatings, creating the precise mil profile required for subsequent coating layers.

  • Mechanical surface preparation
  • Oxidation and rust removal
  • Mill scale stripping
  • Precise adhesion profiling

Beyond protection, we engineer the tactile and visual properties of the surface. We can control gloss levels precisely and apply specific textures to improve grip, reduce glare, or meet strict architectural aesthetic requirements.

  • High and low gloss finishes
  • Matte and satin control
  • Textured and wrinkled finishes
  • Custom appearance matching

For extreme environments, we offer specialized wet coatings, epoxies, and fluoropolymers. These systems are designed specifically to withstand harsh chemical exposure, extreme pH levels, and continuous abrasive contact.

  • Aggressive chemical resistance
  • High-impact abrasion protection
  • Extreme corrosion control
