Marine Sector Solution

High-Salinity Corrosion Defense

The Challenge: A global manufacturer of heavy-duty marine diesel engines was experiencing premature coating failure on their fuel injection pump housings due to extreme salt spray and continuous heat cycling.

The Solution: We engineered a specialized three-stage process: SP-10 abrasive blasting, followed by a proprietary zinc-epoxy barrier primer, and finished with a high-temperature resistant, chemical-impervious polyurethane topcoat.
