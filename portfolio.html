Performance Case Study

How our engineering team solved a critical premature corrosion failure for marine propulsion systems operating in aggressive North Sea environments.

Marine Sector Engineering

High-Salinity Corrosion Defense

The Operational Challenge: A premier manufacturer of heavy-duty marine diesel engines experienced premature coating failure and pitting on ductile iron fuel injection pump housings within 6 months of North Sea offshore service due to concentrated salt spray and continuous 180°C thermal cycling.

The Engineered Solution: TitanCoat engineered a specialized three-stage process: automated SSPC-SP 10 abrasive grit blasting with a verified 2.5-mil angular anchor profile, application of an 85% zinc-rich epoxy cathodic primer, followed by a thermal-shock resistant, chemical-impervious 2K aliphatic polyurethane barrier topcoat.

Substrate: Ductile Iron (ASTM A536)
Coating System: Zinc Epoxy + 2K Polyurethane Topcoat
Operating Environment: ISO 12944 CX Marine (-20°C to 210°C)
Verified Testing: ASTM B117 & ASTM D3359 (5B Adhesion)
