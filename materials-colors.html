Substrate Considerations

Substrate Solution: SP-10 Blast + Zinc-Rich Primer

Carbon Steel Processing

Steel is highly susceptible to rapid oxidation (flash rusting) when exposed to moisture. Proper coating requires complete removal of mill scale and immediate application of a zinc-rich primer.

Pre-treatment Requirement

Mechanical abrasive blasting (SP-10) is typically required to remove mill scale and establish a 1.5 - 2.5 mil anchor profile.

Coating System

Two-coat system recommended: Epoxy primer for corrosion resistance followed by a polyester topcoat for UV stability.

Substrate Solution: Non-Chrome Zirconium Pre-Wash

Aluminum Processing

Aluminum forms a natural oxide layer that can interfere with coating adhesion. It requires specific chemical conversion processes rather than aggressive mechanical blasting.

Pre-treatment Requirement

Multi-stage chemical wash including an acid etch and a non-chrome conversion coating (zirconium based).

Coating System

Single-coat architectural polyester is often sufficient, providing excellent adhesion and weatherability.

Substrate Solution: High-Temp Pre-Bake + OGF Primer

Cast Iron Processing

Cast iron is extremely porous and holds trapped gases that can cause "outgassing" (bubbles/pinholes) during the thermal curing process.

Pre-treatment Requirement

Requires a pre-bake cycle at temperatures higher than the cure temperature to release trapped gases prior to coating.

Coating System

Specialized OGF (Outgas Forgiving) primers or powder formulations are necessary to bridge pores smoothly.

Substrate Solution: SP-7 Brush Blast + Duplex Protection

Galvanized Steel Processing

Galvanized (zinc-coated) steel presents adhesion challenges due to passivators applied at the mill and the smoothness of the zinc layer.

Pre-treatment Requirement

Light sweep blast ("brush off") to profile the zinc without removing it, followed by specialized chemical cleaning.

Coating System

Requires outgas-forgiving powders to prevent bubbling from the porous zinc layer during curing.
