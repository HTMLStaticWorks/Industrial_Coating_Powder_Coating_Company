Formulations & Chemistries

Types of Coatings Offered

Engineered powder, liquid, and protective coating formulations tailored to specific environmental, thermal, and mechanical performance requirements.

AAMA 2604 / 2605

Thermoset Powder Coatings

Super-Durable Polyester TGIC • Pure Epoxy • Hybrids

Electrostatic application forming an impermeable, cross-linked polymer shield. Delivers extraordinary UV stability, salt spray resistance, and color retention without VOC emissions.

Film Thickness 2.0 – 5.0 mils (50 – 125 µm)
Cure Temperature 375°F – 400°F (10–15 min)
Corrosion Defense 1,500 – 3,000+ hrs Salt Spray

Architectural Extrusions Vehicle Wheels & Chassis Electrical Housings

Thermoplastic Coatings

Nylon 11 & 12 • Polyethylene (PE) • PVC Plastisols

Heavy-build protective polymer coatings applied via fluidized bed or specialized electrostatic spray. Provides extreme stone-chip absorption, dielectric insulation, and salt water immersion protection.

Film Thickness 8.0 – 35.0 mils (200 – 900 µm)
Application Method Fluidized Bed / Electrostatic
Special Property High Dielectric & Impact Shield
Common Uses
Battery Trays Wire Goods & Baskets Subsea Clamps
Ambient & Heat Sensitive

High-Performance Liquid Coatings

2K Aliphatic Polyurethane • High-Build Epoxy Enamel

Specialized multi-component liquid systems for heat-sensitive assemblies, oversized structural weldments, or parts requiring on-site field touch-ups and maximum gloss retention.

Film Thickness 3.0 – 12.0 mils (75 – 300 µm)
Cure Mechanism Air / Forced Ambient Reaction
Chemical Resistance Oils, Fuels, Acids & Solvents
Common Uses
Assembled Machinery Crane Booms Transformers & Tanks
Cathodic Protection

Zinc-Rich Primers & Barriers

Formulated with up to 90% zinc dust in the dry film to deliver active galvanic protection. Even if the outer coat is scratched down to bare metal, the zinc sacrificially corrodes to stop rust spread.

Service Category ISO 12944 C5-M Marine Rating

Mild & Carbon Steel

SP-10 Grit Blast

Hot-rolled, cold-rolled, and tubular steel sections. Steel requires aggressive mechanical media blasting to remove mill scale and create an optimal anchor tooth pattern before applying zinc primers.

Anchor Profile: 1.5 – 2.5 mils (38–64 µm)
Recommended Primer: Zinc-Rich Epoxy Powder
Key Benefit: Maximum Adhesion & Rust Block

Aluminum Alloys

Zirconium Wash

Extrusions (6063/6061), sheets (3003/5052), and cast aluminum (A380). Requires a non-chrome conversion wash rather than abrasive blasting to passivate natural oxides and ensure long-term paint bonding.

Pre-Treatment: Multi-Stage Chemical Etch
Conversion Chemistry: Non-Chrome Zirconium
Key Benefit: Zero Outgassing, Superior UV Life

Stainless Steel

Grit Sweep Profile

Grades 304, 316, 316L, and duplex alloys. Stainless has a naturally passive chrome-oxide layer that requires a light, non-ferrous abrasive sweep (aluminum oxide/garnet) to prevent iron contamination and establish mechanical grip.

Blasting Media: Virgin Garnet / Al₂O₃
Surface Profile: 1.0 – 1.5 mils anchor profile
Key Benefit: Prevents Inter-Granular Rusting

Cast Iron & Ductile Iron

Thermal Pre-Bake

Porous iron castings, engine blocks, pump volutes, and valves. Due to trapped air and machining oils in the microscopic voids of cast iron, parts must undergo a high-temperature pre-bake cycle before powder application.

Pre-Bake Cycle: 425°F (218°C) Degassing
Coating Formulation: OGF (Outgas-Forgiving) Primer
Key Benefit: Eliminates Pinholes & Blisters

Galvanized & Galvannealed

Brush-Off Blast

Hot-dip galvanized structural members and electro-galvanized sheet metal. Requires careful brush-off blasting to profile the zinc coating without penetrating it, followed by degassing cure cycles.

Prep Method: SP-7 Brush-Off Blast
Duplex Protection: Zinc Layer + Powder Barrier
Key Benefit: Synergistic 25+ Year Service Life

Brass, Bronze & Copper

Deoxidation Wash

Yellow metals and electrical busbars requiring transparent tarnish-prevention powders, dielectric high-voltage insulation, or specialized low-cure decorative powder coatings.

Pre-Treatment: Deoxidizing Chemical Neutralizer
Coating Types: Clear UV Acrylic / Dielectric Epoxy
Key Benefit: Non-Tarnish Optical Clarity
