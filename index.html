Substrate Compatibility

The Surface Starts with the Substrate.

We engineer coating solutions specifically tailored to the unique metallurgical properties of your base material to ensure maximum bond strength.

Steel

High structural integrity, requires aggressive prep and anti-corrosion base.

Aluminum

Lightweight, demands specific pre-treatment to prevent oxidation issues.

Stainless Steel

Corrosion resistant, needs profiling for mechanical adhesion.

Cast Iron

Porous structure, requires outgassing control during cure cycle.
